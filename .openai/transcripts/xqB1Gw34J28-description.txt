Actor–Network Theory (ANT) explains how our world is shaped by webs of relationships between people, technologies, materials, and ideas. In this video, we break down ANT in simple terms, show how human and non‑human “actors” co‑create outcomes, and walk through real examples from fast fashion to Uber and sustainable architecture. We also cover the main criticisms, so you can use ANT as a lens without falling into endless mapping.

What you’ll learn:

- What ANT is and why it matters
- How agency is distributed across humans and non‑humans
- Practical examples you can spot in everyday life and design work
- The biggest critiques and how to think around them
